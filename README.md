## About the project 
skillproof is a proposed platform designed to help graduates demonstrate their abilities to potential employers through practical challenges, project evidence and structure skills assessments
## The Problem 
graduates can struggle to secure employment when they lack formal work experience even when they have relevant knowledge and skills. Traditional application processes may not always provide enough opportunities for candidates to demonstrate what they can do
## Proposed solution 
skillproof explores a skill-based approach to graduate employability. It aims to help graduates showcase practical abilities and give employers a structured way to evaluate evidence of those abilities 
## Planned features 
- graduate profiles showcasing skills, qualifications and projects
- practical challenges related to specific job skills
- structured assessment criteria for employers
- a demonstration of how skills based evaluation could support recruitment
## Intended users
- recent graduates and entry-level job seekers
- employers and recruiters
- educational institutions supporting graduate employability
## Project status
this project is currently in the concept and prototype development stage. It's assumptions,  feasibility and potential impact will need to be investigated and tested.
## Technology 
the technology stack will be confirmed during prottype development
## Limitations 
skillproof is not intended to guarantee employment. Practical assessments may have limitations, and fair evaluation, accessibility,privacy and verification of submitted work must be considered
