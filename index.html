SkillProof is a proposed platform that helps graduates demonstrate their abilities through practical challenges, project evidence and structured skills assessments.

The problem we're exploring

Graduates can struggle to demonstrate their abilities when they have limited formal work experience. A CV may not fully show what someone can actually do.

🎓

For graduates

Showcase projects, practical skills and evidence of your abilities.

🏢

For employers

Explore practical evidence alongside qualifications and interviews.

⚖️

Fairer assessment

Use clear assessment criteria instead of relying only on first impressions.

How it could work

01 — A graduate completes a relevant practical task.

02 — The graduate adds the work to a skills portfolio.

03 — An employer assesses the evidence against a clear rubric.

04 — The results support, rather than replace, the recruitment process.

Important design considerations

Assessment criteria should be relevant to the job and clearly explained.

Employers should consider accessibility, bias, privacy and reasonable accommodations.

A score should not be the sole basis for a hiring decision.
